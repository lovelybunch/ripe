---
role: marketer
title: Marketer
persona: a marketer in their second week, about to write copy that goes out under the company's name
questions:
  - id: positioning
    weight: 2
    ask: >
      What is our current positioning statement, and which claims are approved
      versus retired? Cite the page that is the source of truth.
    must_cover:
      - One current positioning statement, quoted or closely paraphrased
      - Names at least one claim we no longer make, or says none are retired
      - Cites the page that owns positioning
      - Whether any two sources disagree on the pitch

  - id: audience
    weight: 1.5
    ask: >
      Who are we talking to? Name the audience segments, the person in each,
      and what they care about.
    must_cover:
      - Named segments, not a generic description
      - A buying persona or title per segment
      - What each segment cares about, in their words if we have them

  - id: voice
    weight: 1.5
    ask: >
      Describe our brand voice with the rules a writer must follow. Quote the
      guidance where it exists.
    must_cover:
      - Named tone rules or a style guide
      - Words or phrases we avoid
      - Cites the source, or says explicitly no voice guidance exists

  - id: competitors
    weight: 1.5
    ask: >
      Which competitors do we position against, and what is the one line we use
      for each?
    must_cover:
      - Names competitors from our own material
      - A one-line differentiator per competitor
      - How recent the competitive information is

  - id: campaigns
    weight: 1.5
    expected_gap: true
    ask: >
      What campaigns ran in the last quarter, and what were the results? Cite
      where the results are recorded.
    must_cover:
      - Names specific campaigns with dates
      - A result or metric per campaign, or an explicit "not measured"
      - Cites where results live

  - id: channels
    ask: >
      Which channels do we use, and which one is currently performing best?
    must_cover:
      - Names the channels in use
      - A performance signal for at least one
      - Distinguishes active channels from planned ones

  - id: proof
    weight: 1.5
    ask: >
      Which customer quotes, logos and case studies are cleared for public use?
    must_cover:
      - Names specific assets
      - Permission status per asset, or flags it unknown
      - Where each asset lives

  - id: calendar
    refusal_ok: true
    ask: What is on the content calendar for the next month, and who owns each item?
    must_cover:
      - Named upcoming items with dates, or an explicit "no calendar exists"
      - An owner per item
---

# What a marketer needs from context

Say the same thing the sales team says, in the company's voice, to the people
the company has actually decided to sell to — and know what worked last time.

The expensive failures here are quiet: copy that uses a retired claim, a quote
the customer never cleared, a segment the company walked away from last
quarter. None of those look wrong on the page. Cite everything, and when the
material disagrees with itself, say so rather than picking one.
