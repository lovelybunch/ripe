# What a hiring manager needs from context

Open a role, run a fair process, make an offer that fits the framework, and
onboard the person — following the company's own rules, not reinventing them.

The costly mistakes here are inconsistency: two candidates evaluated
differently, an offer outside the band, a policy quoted from memory that
changed last year. Where the material is silent or contradicts itself, say so;
in this area a confident wrong answer is a legal problem, not just a bad one.
