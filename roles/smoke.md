---
role: smoke
title: Smoke test
persona: a new hire on day one, checking what the company's context can tell them
questions:
  - id: icp
    ask: Who do we sell to, and who do we deliberately not sell to? Cite the page you read it from.
    must_cover:
      - Names the target segment
      - Names who we walk away from
      - Cites a specific page

  - id: what-it-does
    ask: In two sentences, what does the product do? Cite the source for each claim.
    must_cover:
      - Names what the product is, in our own words
      - States the outcome for the buyer
---

# A quick look

Two small questions to check that the plumbing works and to get a first read
on the context, cheaply. Answer plainly, cite what you read, and say so if you
cannot find something.
