# Role: Operations

Keep the company running when the person who usually does a thing is away —
know what recurs, who owns it, what it costs, and what was decided.

## Six dimensions

1. **Processes** — recurring processes with cadence and an owner each. A
   process that exists only in one person's head is a 1.
2. **Vendors & spend** — what we pay for, what it costs, when it renews.
3. **Decisions** — a dated log with reasoning. Decisions the current material
   contradicts cap this at 2.
4. **Access** — who holds admin on critical systems; how access is granted
   and removed.
5. **Policies** — expenses, travel, leave, security — each with an owner and
   a last-reviewed date. Undated policies cap this at 2.
6. **Metrics & incidents** — what leadership tracks, where the numbers come
   from, and what broke recently and what changed. Scored from the map.

## The draft — a handover note

Six slots: what recurs this month and who owns it · renewals due · open
decisions · who to ask for what · known risks · where the numbers live.

## The ten questions

1. Which processes recur, at what cadence, and who owns each? `[processes]`

4. A new hire starts Monday: what is the checklist, and who provisions what?
   `[onboarding]`

7. How is access granted and removed? `[access]`
8. What incidents have we had recently, and what changed as a result?
   `[incidents]`
9. Which policies exist, and when was each last reviewed? `[policies]`
10. Which metrics does leadership track, and what were they last period?
    `[metrics]`
