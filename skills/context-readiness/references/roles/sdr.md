# Role: SDR (Sales Development)

Describe what we sell in one breath, know who to say it to, send materials
that don't come back for a rewrite, and answer inbound questions correctly.

## Six dimensions

1. **Products & pricing** — every offering named; pricing posture stated.
   "Custom — talk to us" is an answer; silence is not. Conflicting prices cap
   this at 2.
2. **Customers & proof** — who we sell to and who we don't; quotes and logos
   with permission noted. Quotes without permission status cap this at 2.
3. **Positioning & competitors** — one current pitch; named competitors with
   when-we-win and when-we-lose.
4. **Materials** — the draft, below.
5. **Answers** — the ten questions, below.
6. **Plumbing & voice** — CRM, call notes and documents connected *with real
   data in them*; an email voice that sounds like us. Scored from the map.

## The draft — a one-pager

Six slots: headline · problem · solution · proof point 1 · proof point 2 ·
pricing-or-CTA line.

## The ten questions

1. What does it do, in two sentences? `[what-it-does]`
2. Who is it for — and who is it *not* for? `[icp]`
3. What does it cost? `[pricing]`
4. How is it different from our main competitor? Name the competitor from our
   own material; no named competitor is itself the finding. `[competitor]`
5. Where does our data live, and who can see it? `[data-residency]`
6. How long does it take to get started? `[getting-started]`
7. What does it integrate with? `[integrations]`
8. Do you have a customer like us? `[proof]`
9. What support do we get? `[support]`
10. Can we talk to a reference? `[reference]`
