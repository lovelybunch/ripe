# Role: People / Hiring Manager

Open a role, run a fair process, make an offer inside the framework, and
onboard the person — following the company's own rules, not reinventing them.

## Six dimensions

1. **Hiring process** — named stages, owners, typical timeline.
2. **Levels & compensation** — a written framework. "Case by case" with no
   bands caps this at 1.
3. **Evaluation** — criteria, disqualifiers, a scorecard. Two candidates
   scored on different criteria is the failure this guards against.
4. **Onboarding** — a first-two-weeks plan with owners and a way to know it
   worked.
5. **Policies** — leave, remote work, expenses, reviews — each dated. Two
   sources stating a policy differently cap this at 2; in this area a
   confident wrong answer is a legal problem.
6. **Organisation & values** — a current org structure and stated values with
   at least one concrete practice each. Scored from the map.

## The draft — a job description

Six slots: the role in one line · what they'll do · what we look for · level
and compensation band · the process and timeline · why us, in our voice.
